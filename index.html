TruthScope

Evidence-first RAG hallucination evaluation

Built an interactive RAG evaluation app with a 60-question benchmark across 51 space-mission documents. Compares baseline, strict Top-3/Top-5, and loose Top-3 retrieval, then checks individual claims against retrieved evidence, flags risky premises, and shows the source chunk. The committed local-simulator benchmark reports hallucination falling from 96.7% to 6.7%.
